# Image prompts (optional ornament layer)

The app is complete without these. Each image is a faint background texture that the app multiplies onto the white paper of the frame at ~14 % strength — it only shows in the header band and the strip under the photo. The app draws all text, prices and the logo itself.

Requirements for every image: square (1024×1024 or larger), **pure white background**, one flat colour, small evenly spaced motifs (so several fit inside a 100 px tall band), no text, no numbers, no logos, no shadows, no gradients.

Save as `assets/frames/pattern-rod.png`, `pattern-gron.png`, `pattern-svart.png`.

## Röd

> A seamless repeating pattern of small hand-drawn grocery line icons — tomato, chili pepper, lemon slice, olive, wheat ear, leaf — evenly scattered with generous spacing, drawn in a single flat red colour (#D10101) with uniform medium line weight on a pure white background. Minimal, friendly, Scandinavian packaging style. Flat vector look, no shading, no gradients, no text, no numbers, no logos, no borders. Square.

## Grön

> A seamless repeating pattern of small delicate olive branches and leaf sprigs, evenly spaced, drawn as flat single-colour silhouettes in deep green (#1A6309) on a pure white background. Calm, fresh, organic, minimalist botanical wallpaper style. Flat vector look, no shading, no gradients, no text, no numbers, no logos, no borders. Square.

## Svart

> A seamless repeating fine geometric pattern inspired by Middle-Eastern tile work — small interlocking eight-point stars and thin lattice lines — drawn with thin uniform black lines (#0E0E0E) on a pure white background. Elegant, premium, restrained, lots of white space between lines. Flat vector look, no shading, no gradients, no text, no numbers, no logos, no borders. Square.
